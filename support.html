App: PDF Compressor: 2MB & More (TinyPDF)
Version 1.0.0 · iOS

What the app does

TinyPDF compresses PDF files to a target size (500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, 5 MB, or a custom size) entirely on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to any server.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my compressed files saved?
Tap Share on the result screen and save to Files, AirDrop, email, or any app you like. The app also keeps a history of recent compressions on your device.

Why can't the app reach my target size?
Some PDFs (mainly scanned images) cannot shrink further without losing readability. The app always tells you when it reached the best possible result.

How many compressions do I get for free?
10 per day, with occasional ads. Pro removes both limits.

I paid for Pro. How do I restore it?
Open the app, tap PRO in the top-right corner, then tap Restore Purchases on the paywall. Use the same Apple ID you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TinyPDF → Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the period ends.
