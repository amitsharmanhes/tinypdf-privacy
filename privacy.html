Privacy Policy

App: PDF Compressor: 2MB & More (TinyPDF)
Last updated: September 9, 2026

Your documents stay on your device

All PDF compression in this app happens entirely on your device. Your PDF files are never uploaded to any server, never transmitted over the internet by us, and never stored anywhere except your own device's storage.

Information we collect

We do not collect, access, or store any personal information. The app does not require an account. We do not receive, view, or store your PDF documents.

Third-party services

Advertising (Google AdMob). The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), usage data, and approximate location data to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

In-App Purchases. All purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for details.

How data is used

Data collected by Google AdMob is used to display advertisements and is not used to identify you personally. No data collected through the app is linked to your identity.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/amitsharmanhes/tinypdf-privacy/issues.
